About

CATTEA began in 2024 as a weekend stall and grew into a small counter off Upper Thomson Road. We keep the list short on purpose: a handful of teas we drink ourselves, brewing ware that lasts, and fancy goods chosen by hand. Everything is packed in small batches, usually the same day it is ordered.

Visit & orders

Counter open Tuesday to Sunday, 11am–7pm. Online orders ship within two working days; collection is available at the counter during opening hours.
